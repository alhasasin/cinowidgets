Our Story

About CINO Bakery & Confectionary

We bake fresh daily with care, serving families, students, and businesses across Zaria.

From a small oven to a city favorite

At CINO Bakery & Confectionary, we started with a simple goal: to deliver warm, delicious baked goods that brighten everyday life. From our humble beginnings in Zaria, we have grown into a trusted local favorite.

Every loaf and snack is crafted with care using quality ingredients and time-tested recipes. We blend traditional baking with modern innovation to create consistent taste and freshness.

Our Mission

To deliver fresh, healthy, and affordable baked goods that make every customer day a little sweeter.

Our Vision

To become the most loved bakery in Nigeria, known for quality, consistency, and community connection.
